Football Edge Privacy Policy

Store Edition • Last updated: 25 September 2026

Football Edge is a sports analytics and live match intelligence application. It does not accept wagers, hold balances, process deposits or withdrawals, execute bets, or connect users to bookmakers.

1. Data used by the app

Football Edge may process the minimum technical data needed to provide live analytics and notifications:

  • push notification registration token supplied by Apple Push Notification service (APNs) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM);
  • a generated device registration identifier used to manage notification delivery;
  • notification preferences and analytical alert thresholds;
  • app version, platform and notification transport status;
  • football fixture, signal, validation and data-quality records used to evaluate analytical performance.

The app does not require a Football Edge user account and does not request payment-card, bank-account, wagering-account or deposit/withdrawal information.

2. Purpose

These data are used only to deliver requested notifications, open the relevant match analysis, maintain service reliability, and evaluate the quality of Football Edge analytical signals.

3. Service providers

Football Edge uses infrastructure and data services required to operate the app, including Cloudflare for application/backend infrastructure, API-Football for football data, and APNs/FCM for push delivery. These providers may process technical network data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Retention and deletion

Device-specific notification registration data is retained while the device remains connected. In the native app, the user can choose Disconnect & Delete Notification Data to remove the server-side notification registration for that device and clear the local Football Edge native device identifier. Invalid notification tokens may also be removed automatically.

De-identified or fixture-level analytical validation records may be retained for product quality, reliability and calibration where they are not used to identify an individual user.

5. Sale, advertising and tracking

Football Edge does not sell personal data. The Store Edition does not include bookmaker affiliate tracking, real-money wagering execution, advertising attribution tracking or cross-app behavioral tracking.

6. Your choices

Push notifications are optional. You can disable notifications in the app or iOS settings. You can also disconnect the current device and delete its Football Edge notification registration from the native notification controls.

7. Support and privacy questions

For privacy or support questions, use the Football Edge Support page included with the app.
